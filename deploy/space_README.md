# SynFinder

Answer four questions and get synthetic data generation methods that actually
fit your study — with the caveats that will bite you, how to evaluate the
output, and every method that was ruled out and why.

69 methods across 10 data types, plus a registry of ready-made synthetic
datasets you may be able to use instead of generating anything.

Source and contributions: https://github.com/sunchang0124/SynFinder
